## Research

### Long COVID Progression Modeling

I am developing a multi-phase state-transition framework to model Long COVID progression by linking acute COVID-19 infection dynamics with post-acute symptom trajectories and longer-term disease burden.

The framework combines population-level infection and recovery data with individual-level simulation to estimate Long COVID incidence, prevalence, symptom trajectories, and persistent disease burden over calendar time.

### Alcohol-Related Disease Microsimulation

I develop individual-level microsimulation models to study how heterogeneous drinking behaviors affect alcohol-related disease progression, mortality, and intervention outcomes across U.S. birth cohorts.

Current applications include:

- Cirrhosis progression and alcohol-attributable mortality
- Esophageal cancer progression
- Interventions for alcohol-use reduction
- Long-term population health effects of treatment duration and effectiveness

### Flu Hospitalization Forecasting

I worked on probabilistic forecasting of seasonal influenza hospitalizations using Google search trends and epidemiological models.

The project included a compartmental SIRS model combined with an Extended Kalman Filter for state estimation and real-time data assimilation.

### Respiratory Motion Modeling

I developed computational approaches for modeling intra-subject respiratory motion using 4D CT imaging and diffeomorphic transformations.

The work involved deformable vector fields, point-cloud representations, image registration, and optimization methods for respiratory motion prediction.

### Airway Tree Segmentation

I worked on deep-learning-based airway segmentation from CT images using the U²-Net architecture and Dice loss, together with post-processing and 3D visualization.
