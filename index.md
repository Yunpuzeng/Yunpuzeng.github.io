## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Industrial Engineering at the Georgia Institute of Technology.

My research focuses on healthcare operations, disease modeling, microsimulation, and data-driven decision making. I am particularly interested in developing computational models to understand disease progression, estimate population-level health burden, and evaluate healthcare interventions.

Outside of research, I enjoy painting, playing the piano, and exploring robotics. 🎨 🎹 🤖
