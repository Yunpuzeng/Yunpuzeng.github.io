### Population-Level Burden of Long COVID in the United States: A Wastewater-Informed Modeling Study

**Yunpu Zeng**, et al.

*Manuscript submitted to **Nature Communications***.

This work develops a population-level framework that combines wastewater-informed estimates of SARS-CoV-2 transmission with Long COVID disease progression modeling to estimate the long-term burden of post-acute COVID conditions in the United States.

### Intra-Subject Respiratory Motion Modeling Based on 4D CT Images via Diffeomorphic Approach

Kunpeng Wang, **Yunpu Zeng**, Zhichun Ye, Zheng Yang, Sai Cheong Fok

*Advances in Information and Communication*, Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems, Springer, 2025, pp. 287–299.

This work develops a patient-specific respiratory motion modeling framework using 4D CT images, deformable vector fields, and a diffeomorphic approach for characterizing lung motion across respiratory phases.

## Preprints

### A CT-Based Airway Segmentation Using U²-Net Trained by the Dice Loss Function

Kunpeng Wang, Yuexi Dong, **Yunpu Zeng**, Zhichun Ye, Yangzhe Wang

*arXiv preprint arXiv:2209.10796*, 2022.

This work applies U²-Net to automatic airway tree segmentation from chest CT scans, using Dice loss to address severe class imbalance between airway and non-airway voxels.
