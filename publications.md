  <div class="pub-status">SUBMITTED · 2026</div>

  <h3>Population-Level Burden of Long COVID in the United States: A Wastewater-Informed Modeling Study</h3>

  <p>
    <strong>Yunpu Zeng</strong>, et al.
  </p>

  <p>
    <em>Manuscript submitted to <strong>Nature Communications</strong>.</em>
  </p>

  <p>
    This work develops a population-level framework that combines wastewater-informed estimates of SARS-CoV-2 transmission with Long COVID disease progression modeling to estimate the long-term burden of post-acute COVID conditions in the United States.
  </p>

  <h3>Intra-Subject Respiratory Motion Modeling Based on 4D CT Images via Diffeomorphic Approach</h3>

  <p>
    Kunpeng Wang, <strong>Yunpu Zeng</strong>, Zhichun Ye, Zheng Yang, Sai Cheong Fok
  </p>

  <p>
    <em>Advances in Information and Communication</em>, Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems, Springer, 2025, pp. 287–299.
  </p>

  <p>
    This work develops a patient-specific respiratory motion modeling framework using 4D CT images, deformable vector fields, and a diffeomorphic approach for characterizing lung motion across respiratory phases.

  <p>
    Kunpeng Wang, Yuexi Dong, <strong>Yunpu Zeng</strong>, Zhichun Ye, Yangzhe Wang
